## Access, identity and lifecycle

### Bosch · Home Connect

- **Access and version.** Home Connect documents a developer client registration and OAuth consent from the user whose appliances are paired. The developer agreement has separate terms for accounts created before and after 2026-09-10. The API documents 1,000 requests per day and 50 per minute per client and user-account pair. A catalog feed, billing unit, fee or quota for bulk catalog acquisition was not verified. [Developer FAQ](https://developer.home-connect.com/support/faq), [authorization](https://api-docs.home-connect.com/authorization), [rate limits](https://api-docs.home-connect.com/general/), [developer agreement](https://developer.home-connect.com/developer_agreement)
- **Identity and lifecycle.** Appliance identifiers and real-time status events belong to the paired-device interface. The review found no catalog-wide product and regional-variant mapping, availability or price/currency fields, versioned product snapshots, or catalog correction and withdrawal signal in that interface. [API overview](https://api-docs.home-connect.com/), [appliance events](https://api-docs.home-connect.com/events)

### Belkin · Channel3

- **Access, cost and version.** Channel3's Belkin page shows keyed `/v1/search`. Public pricing lists 1,000 free credits per month, one credit per search, and USD 0.007 per additional credit. Product detail and webhook subscription endpoints are listed as free. These are advertised API charges, with no project account, account-specific limits or purchased capacity verified. The publisher terms are effective 2026-11-02. [Belkin API](https://www.trychannel3.com/product-data/brands/belkin), [API pricing](https://channel3.ai/docs/pricing), [Publisher Terms](https://www.trychannel3.com/terms/publishers)
- **Identity and prices.** `/v1/products/{product_id}` documents provider IDs, images, variants and merchant offers. Locale parameters include country, language and currency. Price is an amount and currency for a merchant listing. The reviewed docs do not establish a durable mapping from Channel3 IDs to Belkin model numbers, GTINs and regional revisions for a specific candidate subset, or comparable pack sizes and tax or shipping treatment across offers. [Product detail](https://channel3.ai/docs/guides/product-detail), [offers](https://channel3.ai/docs/guides/offer)
- **Freshness, corrections and withdrawals.** Channel3 recommends refreshing price and availability at display time and documents webhook events for price, availability and offer additions/removals. An offer removal reflects a merchant listing change. Belkin-source timestamps, completeness, a product-level correction/deprecation feed and the process for notifying this pilot of a rights withdrawal remain unverified. [Product detail](https://channel3.ai/docs/guides/product-detail), [price updates](https://channel3.ai/docs/guides/webhooks/price), [availability updates](https://channel3.ai/docs/guides/webhooks/availability), [new and removed offers](https://channel3.ai/docs/guides/webhooks/offers), [Publisher Terms §§9, 12](https://www.trychannel3.com/terms/publishers)

### BLUETTI · brand site and US affiliate program

- **Access, cost and version.** The affiliate site describes a free application and advertises promotion services. The API's approval conditions, endpoints, field schema, usage fees, quotas and release version were not verified. The separate brand-site terms are effective 2026-09-20 and apply to that website. [US affiliate program](https://www.bluettipower.com/pages/affiliate-program), [brand-site terms arts. 1–2](https://bluetti.com/terms-of-service)
- **Identity and lifecycle.** The brand-site terms describe informational specifications that can vary by product revision or region, and the site offers no checkout. Exact model/region keys and price/currency/unit fields for a feed, source timestamps, change signals and correction or withdrawal obligations were not verified. Affiliate commissions describe referrals and do not establish product prices or API charges. [Brand-site terms art. 2](https://bluetti.com/terms-of-service), [US affiliate program](https://www.bluettipower.com/pages/affiliate-program)
