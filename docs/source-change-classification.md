# Synthetic source-change classification (CP-11.02 draft)

This isolated, offline primitive compares two **synthetic** observations for the exact same source and material. It records both immutable observation identities, capture instants, content digests, parser revisions and all original field claims. It produces a draft `change-set-draft-0.1.0` review routing result; it never fetches, authorizes reuse, grants rights, edits records, decides a source is removed, or publishes a release.

`src/changes/classify` (exported as `from src.changes import classify`) accepts two dictionaries conforming to `$defs.observation` in `schemas/change-set.schema.json`. Field path IDs must be unique within each observation; schema and semantic validation fail closed for malformed values, unexpected kinds, cross-source comparisons, reused observation IDs, non-UTC dates and reversed capture chronology. Capture timestamps use the exact UTC `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ` grammar with an optional fractional second of one through six ASCII digits. Greater precision is rejected in both the schema and runtime before comparison; no timestamp is rounded or truncated. Decimal price amounts are **strings** and require explicit currency and billing unit. All constrained scalar grammars require full-string matches, including rejection of trailing CR/LF; inputs are never trimmed or normalized. All identities are clearly synthetic. Production inputs are rejected.

The result carries ordered `cause_flags` distinguishing raw-byte changes, parser revisions, semantic claim differences and same-byte/changed-claim conflicts. Changed claims retain full `before` and `after` copies; added and missing claims are not automatically interpreted as source additions or deletions. Alias/identity, availability/retirement and price/unit transitions route to critical review; rights/license changes take precedence and hold future operations; evidence and unknown categories remain visible review items. A parser-only or byte-only change also requires inspection rather than silently declaring cosmetic safety or triggering an unrestricted source refetch. Even unchanged results cannot authorize publication.

Evidence lineage is assessed independently of claim kind. Any changed `evidence_ref`, including a reference on an added or missing claim, adds `evidence` to `impact_classes` while retaining the affected claim kinds. Each change carries its primary `review_route` and an explicit `review_requirements` array. A reference-only change on a non-rights claim has `review` priority and the primary `evidence_lineage_review` route. A simultaneous identity/value/kind change retains its ordinary primary route and priority and also requires `evidence_lineage_review`. Rights/license changes always retain their primary hold route and `rights_hold` decision, including reference-only changes, while keeping any evidence review requirement visible. These are proposed review requirements, not completed reviews or rights decisions.

Run locally, without network or credentials: `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` from the repository root after installing `requirements-validation.txt`. The synthetic fixtures exercise conflicts, denial, price meter drift, malformed values, parser drift, duplicate paths, supported and rejected timestamp precision, trailing line terminators, evidence-only and mixed changes, rights-hold precedence and observation preservation.

**Not complete:** #54 still requires accepted predecessors #53, #35 and #21 (CP-11.01, CP-07.03, CP-04.04); actual source/material/use decisions, historical observation/evidence receipts, real parser/change qualification, review queue ownership, independent approval and receiving evidence remain absent. This module is neither a production source detector nor a source-rights engine. The frozen builder v1 contract and upstream research authority are unchanged.
